- **@insanesurfer** ("How Pat O'Connell SECRETLY Lives Today", 13:04): 118 cuts, **avg shot 6.6s**. Biography/"how X lives now" format. Teal grunge-texture background with **cut-out photos** of the person on it, **screenshots of text with yellow highlighter** on the key line (Wikipedia/AI-answer style cards), name in big white+red type ("ROB MACHADO"), small surf clip inset beside a cut-out figure. Tragedy/danger stories. Needs the most real footage.
- **@surfingstories** ("The Wave You Can Ride For 37 Minutes", 9:51): 187 cuts, **avg shot 3.2s** - the fastest.
