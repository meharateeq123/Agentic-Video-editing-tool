Do not build a film out of video-game capture, even when the film is *about* a game. On the Cleetus / NASCAR 26 video (2026-09-06) an early pass had Dev Diary screen capture under seven of nineteen scenes — about 30% of the running time — and the user rejected it: "tumnai itni ziada game clips lagai hai mainai skhti sai mana kia tha". Only the single reveal shot the script is actually about was kept; every other scene moved to real broadcast footage, taking game footage down to roughly 8%.

For the **next** video the user tightened it further: "next time koi game clip na ho, srf real clip related" — zero game footage, real clips only.

**Why:** a cut full of game capture reads as a game trailer, not as a story about a person. The real footage is what makes the subject a driver rather than a character.

**How to apply:** when picking beds, treat game/screen-capture clips as a single punctuation mark reserved for the exact moment the narration points at it, and source everything else from real broadcast or event footage. If the real-footage pool is too thin to cover the film, download more real footage rather than padding with game capture. See [[flick-footage-pacing]] and [[reddick-southern-500-film]].
