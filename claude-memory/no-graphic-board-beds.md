**Why:** a 9:16 centre crop of a 16:9 graphic shows half-words and no subject; it reads as a wrong/irrelevant clip.
**How to apply:** for every bed under a named-person line use a clear shot of THAT person (face visible, ideally with their name label); use a result/lane/title graphic only if the crop shows it fully readable.
