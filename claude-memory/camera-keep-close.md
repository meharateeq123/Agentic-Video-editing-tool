On 2026-09-30 the user said of Laplace scenes 6–7: "things are very far from the camera, you can't see them". Those shots used a frame height of 15 so they could show both worlds at once.

**Why:** at height 15 the equations and the tub were tiny. A 16:9 frame at height ~11 is already ~20 units wide, which is enough for the whole stage.

**How to apply:**
- Keep the camera frame height at about 12 or less.
- If a beat needs two far-apart things in shot, move them closer together. Don't widen the shot.
- Formulas that must be read stay large: size ~60 or more for key lines.
