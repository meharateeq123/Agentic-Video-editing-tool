On richardson1 the user's verdict was "impressive work laikin relevancy rh gai" (2026-09-18) — the
build was fine, the shot-to-line match was not.

**Why:** in that short several beats ran footage that was merely *about the same person* rather than
about the thing being said — Brussels close-ups under a Budapest story, two Americans on screen
while the line was about Julien Alfred, and Brussels b-roll under "heading to Athlos in London".
Each one was a conscious compromise, and each was flagged in the delivery note. Flagging it does not
fix it; the viewer only sees the mismatch.

**How to apply:** before baking a bed, say out loud what the line claims and what the shot shows. If
they are not the same thing, the answer is to go find the right footage, not to use the near-miss.
- A line naming a person needs that person on screen.
- A line naming a meet, race or venue needs that meet on screen.
- A line about something with no footage yet (an event that has not happened) is a type/stamp beat
  over a neutral shot — not b-roll of a different event pretending to be it.
- If the right footage genuinely does not exist, raise it before building, not after rendering.
