**How to apply:** when proposing or writing a ManimGL script, meet this bar:
1. The first line is a claim that sounds impossible.
2. The answer is withheld until the end, and every scene opens a new question.
3. There are real stakes: money or prizes, lives, fraud, madness, war, a real person's fate.
4. The math contradicts intuition.
5. The ending reframes how the viewer sees something.

Avoid school-lesson topics and over-made topics unless there is a fresh angle.
