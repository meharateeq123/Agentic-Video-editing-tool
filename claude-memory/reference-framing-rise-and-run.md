User (2026-10-16) told me to download https://www.youtube.com/shorts/FmOoOSIWDwg and learn frame by frame how it shows the subject so well. Saved at `D:\flick\output\shorts\refs\FmOoOSIWDwg\` (ref.mp4, sheet_2fps.png, four.png).

What it does (19.7s, 8 shots):
- **Subject fills the frame.** Tight vertical crop centred on HER: body 50-75% of frame height, head in the upper third, feet usually in. Never a thin band of runners over empty track. Only one wide shot (the finish tape with the crowd), and even there the runners sit in the lower half with the crowd as context, not empty infield.
- **Long continuous shots, 2-4s each**, one real moment per line: race start → mid-race → finish tape → celebration close-up (bib RICHARDSON readable) → another finish → slow-motion tracking close-up → interview face to close.
- **Slow motion** on the run and tracking shots makes the close crops sharp and dramatic.
- **Transitions:** a radial zoom-blur punch into a close-up, and one short double-exposure blend; otherwise hard cuts on the word.
- **Interview close-up**: face centred, fills the frame width, mic at the bottom.
- **Subtitles:** one word at a time, YELLOW (~#FFE14D) bold italic uppercase, thick black outline + shadow, at ~75% height.
- Footage is colour-punched (contrast/saturation up, sharpened).
- No graphics or stamps in this one; the user separately asked for beautiful motion graphics on the 2026-10-16 shorts, so add them on top without breaking the framing.

**How to apply:** per bed choose cx/cy/s so the named person is centred and large (s ≈ 0.45-0.6 of source height on 1080p sources), check every bed's first/middle/last frame, prefer close tracking/slow-mo sources, and use yellow one-word subs.
