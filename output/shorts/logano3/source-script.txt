Joey Logano DOMINATED Gateway... Then LOST!

Joey Logano looked unstoppable at Gateway. He started on pole, won Stage 1, and led a race-high 96 laps. But Kyle Larson's late strategy helped change everything. Logano finished third instead of taking the win, leaving him with a huge "what if?" Now Bristol is next, and Logano is still 51 points behind Hamlin. Could he finally strike back?
